The element counter is not a byte, RSS or cumulative CPU allowance. Failed parses do not spend it; nested Packages have separate counters; direct XML paths keep their own per-part limits. Optional part refusal can omit formatting/content, and some callers do so silently. Two nonblocking review follow-ups remain: inaccurate serialization/collision comments, and consistent warnings for optional-part loss. No full Office parity, full security audit, issue closure, Windows11/macOS Intel validation, private-host integration or complete new offline distribution is established by this change.
